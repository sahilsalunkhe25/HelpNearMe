# HelpNearMe

### 🔧 A community-powered platform to find trusted local helpers like electricians, plumbers, tutors, and more — all based on your pincode.

---

## 🚀 What is HelpNearMe?

HelpNearMe is a **simple, no-login web app** designed to make it easier for people in India to find local help — and just as importantly, to help others in their area **earn a living**.

Most existing platforms ask users to sign up, book appointments, and pay fees — even for small tasks. HelpNearMe skips all that.

Just:
- ✅ Enter your **pincode**
- ✅ See real listings (electricians, tutors, mechanics, etc.)
- ✅ Filter by type of help
- ✅ View ratings, reviews, and **call directly**
- ✅ Add a helper you trust using a quick form — only phone number and pincode needed

---

## 🌟 Why it matters

1. **Helps people quickly find help in their area**
2. **Supports local workers** — especially those who rely on word-of-mouth
3. **Builds community-driven trust**, one pincode at a time

## 🧑‍💻 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React.js  
- **Backend**: Spring Boot  
- **Database**: PostgreSQL  
- **Hosting**: Vercel (Frontend) + Railway/Supabase (Backend/DB)
